# Task: Build Phase 1 - Public Website Layout & Home Page

## Project Context

This project is the official website for the **5th Coast Guard Global Summit (CGGS 2027)**, hosted by the **Indian Coast Guard** under the **Ministry of Defence, Government of India**.

This is an international government summit website, not a defence portal or corporate website.

The design language should communicate:

- Authority
- Prestige
- International Collaboration
- Maritime Excellence
- Premium Government Experience

The experience should feel comparable to:

- World Governments Summit
- World Economic Forum
- COP Summit
- G20 Summit

while maintaining a unique Indian Coast Guard identity.

# Motion & Interactions

Create a premium interactive experience.

Use smooth motion throughout the homepage.

Examples include

- Scroll reveal animations
- Fade and slide transitions
- Smooth page transitions
- Hover interactions
- Animated counters
- Timeline animations
- Carousel transitions
- Floating decorative elements
- Section entrance animations

Include tasteful parallax effects where appropriate.

Animations should feel elegant and premium.

Avoid excessive or distracting effects.

# Wow Sections

The homepage should contain at least one or two memorable sections that create a strong first impression.

Examples may include:

- An immersive hero experience
- A dynamic timeline
- Interactive world map highlighting participating nations
- Animated maritime-themed visual section
- Premium statistics showcase
- Scroll-driven storytelling
- Elegant ship or ocean-inspired transitions

Use creativity to make the homepage feel unique and worthy of an international government summit.
