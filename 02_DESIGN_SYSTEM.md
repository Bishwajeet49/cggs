# CGGS 2027 – Design System

> Version: 1.0
> Applies to: Public pages & Delegate Portal pages

---

# Design Vision

The CGGS 2027 website should represent an international government summit hosted by the Indian Coast Guard.

The experience should communicate:

- Authority
- Trust
- Prestige
- Maritime Identity
- International Collaboration
- Modern Government Technology

The UI should feel comparable to platforms such as:

- World Governments Summit
- World Economic Forum
- COP Summit
- G20 Summit

while maintaining a unique Indian Coast Guard identity.
